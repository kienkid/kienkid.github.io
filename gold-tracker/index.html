Methodology & notes

  • DOJI: daily closing sell price of DOJI Nhẫn tròn 9999 (Hưng Thịnh Vượng) in VND per tael, source: giavang24k.com. Days are stored as published (including weekends).
  • XAU/USD: daily close in USD per troy ounce (Stooq; Yahoo GC=F futures used only as fallback). Only completed sessions are stored.
  • USD/VND: Vietcombank selling rate.
  • Converted price: XAU/USD × USD/VND × 37.5 / 31.1034768 (1 tael = 37.5 g, 1 troy oz = 31.1034768 g). Excludes taxes, fabrication costs and dealer margin.
  • Weekends/holidays: the last available XAU/USD and USD/VND values are carried forward; those points are marked “carried forward” in tooltips.
  • History: data from 11/06/2026 is frozen; one new day is appended every evening at about 21:00 (GMT+7).
  • For information only – not investment advice.
Data: DOJI prices via giavang24k.com · Stooq · Vietcombank. Updated daily via GitHub Actions.
